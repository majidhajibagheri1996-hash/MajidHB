# TV background photos

Every `.jpg`, `.png` or `.webp` image in this folder becomes a background on the TV
when **Customize → Background → My photos (from phone)** is selected in the TVHome app.
Several images rotate as a slideshow.

Upload from a phone: open this folder on github.com (switch to the
`ccr-926cf38e-r05h1c` branch), tap **Add file → Upload files**, pick the photos and
tap **Commit changes**. The TV picks them up within 30 minutes, or right away when
the app is reopened.

This repository is public, so anything uploaded here can be seen by anyone.
Landscape photos around 1920×1080 look best.
